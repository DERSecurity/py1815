**The master can break a protocol rule on purpose, to test how an outstation answers what the standard says it must refuse (#89).** A deviation is named and off by default, and is applied to the octets the master built on their way out, so with every one off the master stays conformant. The ten are `silence`, `repeat_request`, `corrupt_header_crc`, `corrupt_body_crc`, `truncate`, `overstate_length`, `break_transport_sequence`, `orphan_segment`, `withhold_confirmation` and `wrong_confirmation_sequence`. Set them in Python on `outstation.deviations`, or over the service and the console with the `deviate` operation. See [Misbehaving on purpose](https://dersecurity.github.io/py1815/master/#misbehaving-on-purpose).
