**An outstation at Subset Level 4 serves double-bit binary inputs (#116).** A point map can hold points of the new kind `DBI`; `Binding.read(Kind.DBI, index, reader)` binds one to a reader that returns its state, 0 to 3. At Level 4 the outstation answers group 3 reads in the packed variation and in the variation with flags (a point in an exception condition always takes the variation with flags), reports state and quality changes as group 4 events without time, with absolute time, or with relative time behind a common time of occurrence, and includes the points in class 0 and in class 1 to 3 reads. Below Level 4 groups 3 and 4 are unknown objects. The Device Profile declares the points, and the master reads, decodes and stores them (`read(double_bit_inputs=...)`, `Store.double_bit_input`, point type `dbi` in the API).
