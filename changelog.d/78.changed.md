**An outstation is settled when it is connected to (#78).** `Master.add` and
`Outstation.connect` used to send nothing; they are now followed by the startup
sequence, and a lost connection is made again. Pass `manual=True` for the master that
sends only what it is asked to, or `tasks=Tasks.none()` to keep confirmations, and
`reconnect=None` to stay disconnected. In a service that was not started with
`--allow-control`, the tasks that clear the restart indication and set the clock are
off, so it changes nothing at an outstation it was only meant to read.
